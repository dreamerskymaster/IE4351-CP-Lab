What is Laser Engraving?
A high-power laser beam removes material through melting, burning, and vaporization — leaving a permanent, high-quality mark. This process is heavily used for part traceability in industry.

⚠️ Lab Safety Rules

Required before entering the lab

Do NOT touch or operate any machine without a lab technician or TA present. Laser radiation is invisible and potentially hazardous to skin and eyes.

👓 Safety Glasses

Required at all times in the lab environment — from the moment you enter.

👟 Closed-Toe Shoes

Leather preferred. Required for lab entry — no exceptions.

💇 Tie Back Hair

Long hair must be secured away from all moving parts before entering.

🚫 No Jewelry

No rings, watches, or dangling accessories when operating equipment.

🔴 Laser Safety Instructions

Invisible radiation — extremely hazardous

⛔ Critical Laser Hazards

  • Laser radiation is invisible — direct or scattered exposure is hazardous to skin and eyes.
  • This laser can produce dangerous diffuse reflections, risking eye injury and fire.
  • Do NOT look at the laser beam continuously, not even through protective glass.
  • Only trained personnel aware of the risks are authorized to use the machine.
Broken Lens Emergency: Switch off the machine → Hold your breath → Leave the area → Wait 30 minutes → Ventilate the room. Report immediately to the TA.

🚦 Indicator Lights and Movement

Reading the machine status and jogging the head

Green Light

ON: Machine is initializing.
OFF: File processing complete, ready to receive a file, or paused.

Red Light

ON: Machine is initializing.
OFF: Ready to receive a file or paused.
FLASHING: Machine is actively engraving.

Joystick Movement

X-Axis
Press and hold left/right arrows on joystick
Y-Axis
Press and hold up/down arrows on joystick
Z-Axis
Press Z key, then use the Y movement keys
Incremental vs. Continuous: Press and immediately release for small, precise movements. Hold the key for continuous movement that accelerates over time.

🖥️ Gravostyle Software

Creating your laser engraving composition

1

Power On: Switch on the PC connected to the machine, then switch on the machine. Wait for start-up to complete.

2

Set Page Size: Adjust the page size in Gravostyle to match the maximum size of the engraving area on the machine.

3

Import the NU Logo: Import the Northeastern University logo file. Set fill to black, contour to none.

4

Add Your Name: Enter your name as a text element. Set to black fill, no contour as well.

5

Position Using Circle Guide: Create a circle the same diameter as the coaster. Position it to match the coaster's location on the table. Place the logo and text inside it. Remove the circle before printing.

📐 Composition Layout

How to position logo and name on the coaster

N
Your Name

Dashed circle = coaster boundary (remove before printing)

  • ✅ Place composition in the top-left corner of the Gravostyle page
  • ✅ Logo and text must have black fill, no contour
  • ✅ Measure the coaster and scale the circle to match exactly
  • ✅ Position the circle to match the coaster location on the table
  • ⛔ Remove the circle before sending the job to the machine
Precision Matters: The top-left corner of the Gravostyle page corresponds to position (0,0) on the physical engraving table. Any offset in software = misplaced engraving on the coaster.

📤 Transferring the File

Sending your composition to the LS900

1

Check USB Connection: Confirm the USB cable is securely connected at both ends — the PC and the rear panel of the machine.

2

Verify Machine Status: The machine's LCD screen must display "Ready to receive" before you attempt to transfer.

3

Click Print in Gravostyle: A status screen shows work completion as a percentage. The file may take several minutes to fully transfer. Wait until the machine display updates before proceeding.

4

Confirm Transfer: Once complete, the machine display shows the file name and key engraving parameters, confirming the job is loaded and ready.

Do Not Rush: If the Gravostyle status screen disappears quickly, the file may still be transferring in the background. Always wait for the machine display to confirm before touching the machine.

🎯 Positioning and Focusing

Placing the coaster and setting the focal height

📍

Position the Coaster

Open the front door. Place the coaster in the top-left corner of the engraving table against the scaled rulers. This position must match the circle location in your Gravostyle composition.

🔍

Focus the Head

Check that the table is low enough that the moving head will not touch the coaster. Press Autofocus to automatically set the focal height. Autofocus runs at each launch by default.

Batch Engraving Tip: If engraving multiple coasters of the same thickness, you can disable autofocus after the first one to save time — focus only runs on the first plate.

💡 Quiz Hint (Z Levels): The laser beam converges to a specific focal point. Moving the Z height away from this optimal distance changes the beam focus, causing the spot size to increase and energy to disperse. Properly focused = deeper, sharper marks; out of focus = lighter, more diffuse marks.

▶️ Engraving Checklist

Seven steps before pressing Start

1

Switch on the air extraction system.

2

Select the engraving mode. Your TA will specify which of the two available modes to use.

3

Confirm the coaster is correctly positioned in the top-left of the engraving area, against the rulers.

4

Check the focus. Press Autofocus if not already done for this coaster.

5

Close and confirm the door is fully shut.

6

Confirm the LCD screen displays the correct file name for your composition.

7

Press the START button. The red light will flash during engraving. Do not open the door.

After Completion: The laser shuts off and the head returns to the origin (top-left). Wait a few seconds for fumes to be fully extracted, then open the door and remove your coaster.

Today's Key Principles:

1. Laser Safety First: Invisible radiation — never bypass safety systems or open covers during operation.

4. Black Fill, No Contour: Correct Gravostyle settings are critical for a clean, consistent engrave.
